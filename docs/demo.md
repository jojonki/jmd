`inline code`, and [links](https://commonmark.org).

> Write your way and stay in sync.

Inline math stays in the flow: $p_t(x)=\int p_t(x\mid z)p_{\mathrm{data}}(z)\,dz$.

Differentiate a time-dependent marginal under the integral sign:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\partial_t p_t(x)
&= \partial_t \int p_t(x\mid z)p_{\mathrm{data}}(z)\,dz.
\end{aligned}
$$
